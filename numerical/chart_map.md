# Chart map

All paper figures and AI Growth Lab charts use the canonical equilibrium outputs
generated by `scripts/simulate_equilibrium.py` and
`scripts/simulate_high_sigma_equilibrium.py`. Levels that grow over several orders
of magnitude are reported as natural-log changes from date zero. Bounded shares and
ratios are shown in levels unless the chart explicitly says otherwise.

## AI Growth Lab grouping

The web laboratory follows the same economic tree: population allocation;
production; household outcomes; integrated developer; output allocation; and AI
research. It does not place levels, rates, and shares in the same chart. The only
log-level ratios are ln(H/M), ln(qA/Y), and ln(qA/K); all bounded shares remain in
their original scale.
