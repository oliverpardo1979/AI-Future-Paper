\title{The Future of Growth and Human Labor under Automated AI Research:\\
AI Capability as Research-Augmenting Technology}
\author{Oliver Pardo}
\date{August 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
This paper develops an endogenous-growth model in which current AI capability
turns compute into both production services and automated research services. A
monopolist supplies AI services and invests in better AI using human researchers
and research compute. Final output combines capital with a CES aggregate of labor
and AI services. If these inputs are complements, production labor remains a
long-run bottleneck: when its population share stays positive, output per person
is constant on a balanced-growth path. At unit elasticity, positive per-capita
growth requires diminishing returns to research to dominate the feedback from
better AI to production and AI research. If the inputs are substitutes and AI
services come to dominate final production, an interior path with unbounded AI
improvement cannot have finite constant growth rates. On a characterized
accelerating path, labor's income share vanishes, but real wages eventually rise
when substitution is below an explicit threshold. Perfect-foresight simulations
illustrate the transition toward the unit-elasticity balanced-growth limits.
\end{abstract}
